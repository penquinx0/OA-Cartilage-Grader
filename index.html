How it works

The cartilage is located by stain colour (red proteoglycan in Safranin-O, violet matrix in H&E), including pale, depleted cartilage next to stained areas. Sixteen 224 px tiles per image are read by a frozen DINOv2 ViT-S/14 network. A ridge regression maps the averaged features, plus two stain measurements, to an OARSI score. With both stains it uses the combined model, which tested best.

How it was tested

Trained on 58 knee compartments from 29 patients, each scored by 3 scorers × 3 reads. Accuracy was measured leaving one patient out at a time, so every test case is a patient the model never saw. MAE is the average gap, in OARSI points, between the estimate and the expert mean.

The browser runs an 8-bit compressed copy of the network. On the training images its estimates differ from the full-precision model by 0.26 points on average.

Held-out accuracy

OARSI grade × stage scoring (0–24). Bands used here: Mild < 5, Moderate 5–8, Severe ≥ 8. Prototype built for a student research project; outputs are estimates and not a clinical assessment.
